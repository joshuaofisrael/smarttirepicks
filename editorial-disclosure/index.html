Editorial Disclosure

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Short answer: Smart Tire Picks publishes original educational guides and editorial overviews. Illustrative photos are labeled as AI generated. Google AdSense ads and any future affiliate commissions do not decide what a page concludes.

Who writes the pages

Guides, comparisons, tools, and reviews are written for Smart Tire Picks, a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. They are original explanations for US drivers. They are not copied manufacturer pages, and a tire brand does not pay for a positive verdict.

What a review is

Review pages are editorial overviews of public manufacturer positioning and the category a tire sits in. Unless a page explicitly says we ran a named test, we have not done laboratory, track, or instrumented testing. Phrases such as “worth a look” are opinion, not a safety ranking. Confirm size, load index, speed rating, and pressure on your door placard and in your owner’s manual, and have tires mounted by a licensed installer. See the Disclaimer.

AI generated images

Photographs used as page illustrations are AI generated. Each one is captioned “Illustrative AI generated photo.” They show a typical tire, wheel, garage, or workshop scene so the page is easier to scan. They are not photos of a specific test, a specific vehicle, or a product we measured. Diagrams that explain rotation patterns are drawings, not photographs.

Ads and commissions

The site may show display ads through Google AdSense. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies. You can opt out of personalized ads at https://adssettings.google.com. How that works is in the Privacy Policy. There are no live affiliate links today. If we add them, the Affiliate Disclosure will say so next to the link. Ads and commissions do not change fitment rules or the conclusions on a page.

Corrections

If a page is wrong or out of date, tell us through the contact form. We read corrections and update the page when the source supports the change.
