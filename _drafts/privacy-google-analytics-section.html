Analytics: Google Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google LLC, to understand how visitors find and use the Site, such as which pages are read and roughly where visits come from. Google Analytics sets cookies (for example _ga and _ga_*) and collects information such as pages viewed, referring site, device and browser type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalization for this property, and we do not use Google Analytics data to identify you.

Google Analytics loads only after you click Accept on our analytics banner. If you click Decline, or ignore the banner, it does not load. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's storage in your browser, which brings the banner back. You can also use the Google Analytics opt out browser add on.

Google processes this data under its own terms; see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Analytics data is retained for 14 months.
