Tire guides

Plain English guides for US drivers, grouped by task, with public NHTSA, USTMA and eCFR citations on each page.

This page is for general information only and is not professional, safety, or legal advice. Always confirm tire size, load index, speed rating, and other specs against your vehicle’s door placard and owner’s manual, and have tires installed by a licensed professional. We have not lab-tested products unless explicitly stated. See our Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

Short answer: start with your vehicle’s door placard. It lists the original tire size and the cold pressures the vehicle maker recommends, and most other tire questions (pressure, TPMS lights, replacement size, load index) build on it. The guides below are grouped by the job you are doing, and the table maps common questions to the page that answers them.

A simple tire check routine

A general habit many drivers use, based on NHTSA consumer guidance to check pressure monthly with a gauge. Your owner’s manual and installer take priority.

WhenWhat to checkGuide
MonthlyCheck cold pressure on all four tires and the spare with a gauge, against the door placard, not the sidewall maximum.Pressure guide
MonthlyLook at tread across the full width of each tire. Wear bars or uneven wear are a reason to look closer.Tread depth guide
When a light comes onNote whether the TPMS light is solid or flashes then stays on, then check pressures with a gauge.TPMS guide
Before buying tiresCopy the placard size and pressures, then match load index and speed rating at or above OEM.Placard checklist
At each seasonal swapRead the DOT date code, inspect for damage, and store the off season set clean, cool and out of sunlight.Storage guide
